# Article 5 Prohibited Practices Statutory Checklist

This checklist operationalizes Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Any positive finding indicates an **unacceptable risk practice** that cannot be placed on the market, put into service, or used within the Union.

| Statutory Provision | Prohibition Description | Legal Thresholds & Required Elements | Exceptions & Derogations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article 5(1)(a)** | Subliminal or purposefully manipulative/deceptive techniques | 1. Deploys subliminal techniques beyond consciousness OR purposefully manipulative/deceptive techniques.<br>2. Objective or effect is to materially distort behavior.<br>3. Appreciably impairs informed decision-making.<br>4. Causes or is reasonably likely to cause significant harm. | None. Purely prohibited. |
| **Article 5(1)(b)** | Exploitation of specific vulnerabilities | 1. Targets age, disability, or specific social/economic situation.<br>2. Materially distorts behavior.<br>3. Causes or is reasonably likely to cause significant harm. | None. |
| **Article 5(1)(c)** | Social scoring of natural persons | 1. Conducted by public authorities OR private actors.<br>2. Evaluates trustworthiness based on social behavior or personality traits.<br>3. Leads to detrimental treatment in unrelated social contexts OR disproportionate/unjustified treatment. | Does not prohibit legitimate credit scoring conducted pursuant to consumer credit laws (governed under Annex III as high-risk). |
| **Article 5(1)(d)** | Individual criminal risk assessment / predictive policing | 1. Assesses or predicts the risk of an individual committing a criminal offence.<br>2. Based solely on profiling or assessing personality traits/past behavior. | Does NOT apply to AI systems supporting human assessment based on objective and verifiable facts directly linked to criminal activity. |
| **Article 5(1)(e)** | Untargeted facial recognition database scraping | 1. Creates or expands facial recognition databases.<br>2. Uses untargeted scraping of facial images from the internet or CCTV footage. | Targeted collection pursuant to lawful warrant or specific criminal investigation. |
| **Article 5(1)(f)** | Emotion recognition in workplace and educational institutions | 1. Used in workplace or educational institutions.<br>2. Infers emotions of natural persons. | Permitted where intended to be put in place for medical or safety reasons (e.g., driver fatigue detection). |
| **Article 5(1)(g)** | Biometric categorisation of sensitive attributes | 1. Categorises individually natural persons based on biometric data.<br>2. Deduces race, political opinions, trade union membership, religious/philosophical beliefs, sex life, or sexual orientation. | Lawful filtering or labelling of acquired biometric datasets in the area of law enforcement. |
| **Article 5(1)(h)** | Real-time remote biometric identification (RBI) in publicly accessible spaces | 1. Used in publicly accessible spaces.<br>2. Real-time operation.<br>3. For law enforcement purposes. | Strictly permitted ONLY for:<br>(i) Targeted search for specific victims of abduction, trafficking, or missing persons;<br>(ii) Prevention of substantial, imminent life threat or genuine/present terror threat;<br>(iii) Localisation/identification of suspects of serious crimes (Annex II) punishable by 4+ years imprisonment.<br>*Requires prior judicial authorization and FRIA.* |
