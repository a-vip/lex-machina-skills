# Global AI Regulatory Mapping Matrix: EU vs. US vs. China vs. CoE

| Governance Dimension | EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689) | US NIST AI RMF 1.0 & GenAI Profile (NIST AI 600-1) | China CAC AI Trilogy (Orders 9, 12, 15) | Council of Europe (CETS No. 225) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Nature** | Legally binding Regulation; direct effect across 27 EU Member States. | Voluntary framework; reinforced by FTC Section 5, EO 14110, and agency rules. | Legally binding administrative regulations with algorithmic registry filings. | Binding international treaty on human rights, democracy, and rule of law. |
| **Risk Taxonomy** | 4 Tiers: Unacceptable (banned), High-risk, GPAI with systemic risk, Specific transparency. | Continuous lifecycle functions: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE + 12 GenAI risk categories. | Graded & classified: Public opinion / social mobilization services trigger security review. | Graduated & differentiated risk management framework (Article 16). |
| **Risk Management System** | Mandatory Article 9: Continuous iterative process across entire lifecycle; annual update. | Core Functions MAP and MANAGE: Ongoing monitoring, TEVV, residual risk determination. | Article 7 & 17: Lifecycle algorithm principle reviews and annual security assessments. | Mandatory Article 16: Identification, assessment, prevention, and iterative mitigation. |
| **Fundamental Rights Review** | Mandatory Article 27: Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA) for public/essential deployers. | MAP 5.1 & MEASURE 2.11: Socio-technical impacts, fairness, systemic bias evaluations. | Article 4: Protection of lawful rights, personal dignity, and non-discrimination. | Article 14 & 16: Accessible remedies and iterative impact assessment on human rights. |
| **Synthetic Media Watermarking** | Article 50(2): Outputs must be marked in a machine-readable format and detectable. | MANAGE 2.2: Cryptographic provenance (C2PA) and synthetic watermarking. | Order 12 Arts. 16–17: Mandatory implicit metadata logging + conspicuous visual labels. | Article 8: Transparency regarding identification of AI-generated content. |
| **Foundation / Generative AI** | Articles 51–55: Dual tier: Baseline transparency/copyright vs. Systemic risk ($>10^{25}$ FLOPs). | NIST AI 600-1: 12 risk categories, red-teaming for CBRN and cyber exploitation. | Order 15: Public generative AI requires algorithm filing, IP compliance, and data consent. | Article 3: Lifecycle application covering public and private actors. |
| **Training Data Copyright** | Article 53(1)(c)–(d): Directive (EU) 2019/790 Art. 4(3) opt-out policy + public content summary. | GOVERN 1.1: Intellectual property and fair use evaluation; dataset licensing audit. | Order 15 Art. 7: Mandatory legitimate source data; non-infringement of IP rights. | Article 4: General principle of respect for human rights and rule of law. |
| **Enforcement & Fines** | AI Office + National Authorities; fines up to **€35M / 7% of turnover**. | FTC enforcement (deceptive practices), DOJ, civil litigation, federal contractor rules. | CAC, MIIT, MPS; suspension of services, rectification orders, administrative penalties. | Follow-up Committee (Article 23) + domestic judicial and administrative enforcement. |
