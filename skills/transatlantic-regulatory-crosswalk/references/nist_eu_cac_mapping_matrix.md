# Global AI Regulatory Mapping Matrix (2026 Edition: EU vs. US Federal/State vs. China vs. CoE)

| Governance Dimension | EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689 + Digital Omnibus 2026/1744) | US Federal (Post-EO 14110 Revocation) & Binding State AI Statutes | China CAC Framework (Orders 9, 12, 15 + Sept 2025 Labeling Measures) | Council of Europe Framework Convention (CETS No. 225) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Nature & 2026 Status** | Directly applicable Regulation across 27 Member States; amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. | **EO 14110 REVOKED Jan 20, 2025.** Voluntary NIST AI RMF 1.0/600-1 + FTC Sec. 5 + binding state statutes (**Texas TRAIGA HB 149 live Jan 1, 2026**; **CA AB 2013 & SB 53/TFAIA**; **CO SB 24-205 / SB 26-189**). | Legally binding administrative regulations + mandatory national standard **GB 45438-2025** (effective Sept 1, 2025). | First legally binding international AI treaty; formally approved/concluded by the EU in mid-2026 alongside US/UK/global signatories. |
| **Prohibited Practices** | Art. 5 (8 initial bans live Feb 2, 2025; **NCII sexual deepfakes & CSAM added effective Dec 2, 2026**). | **Texas TRAIGA (live Jan 1, 2026)**: bans intentional self-harm incitement, social scoring, unlawful biometric capture, intentional discrimination. | Order 15 Art. 4 & Order 12 Art. 6: bans unlawful/subversive outputs, fake news synthesis, unauthorized biometric editing. | Art. 16(4): Parties must assess need for bans or moratoria incompatible with human rights, democracy, or rule of law. |
| **High-Risk & Risk Management** | Art. 9 RMS & Annex III (postponed by Omnibus to **Dec 2, 2027**; Annex I products **Aug 2, 2028**). | NIST AI RMF (`GOVERN`/`MAP`/`MEASURE`/`MANAGE`) acts as safe harbor / rebuttable presumption in Colorado AI Act. | Order 9/15 Arts. 7 & 17: Mandatory security assessments & Algorithm Filing Registry for public-opinion AI. | Art. 16: Graduated, differentiated iterative risk and impact management framework across public & private actors. |
| **Synthetic Content Labeling** | Art. 50(2) machine-readable marking **live Aug 2, 2026** (legacy GenAI cutoff **Dec 2, 2026**). | NIST AI 600-1 `MANAGE 2.2` (C2PA provenance) + state deepfake & election disclosure laws. | **Sept 1, 2025 Labeling Measures + GB 45438-2025**: binding explicit labels + implicit metadata across entire supply chain. | Art. 8: Identification of AI-generated content and user interaction transparency. |
| **Frontier / GPAI & Copyright** | Arts. 51-55 **live Aug 2, 2025** (legacy grace Aug 2, 2027) + July 2025 GPAI Code of Practice (`>10^25` FLOPs systemic risk). | **California AB 2013** (training data transparency) & **SB 53 / TFAIA** (frontier safety frameworks, incident reporting, whistleblowers). | Order 15 Art. 7: Mandatory legitimate training sources, IP non-infringement, personal data consent. | Art. 3 & Art. 9: Accountability and human-rights lifecycle compliance. |
| **Enforcement & Penalties** | EU AI Office + National Authorities: up to **EUR 35M / 7% turnover** (Art. 99(3)). | Exclusive **State Attorneys General** (TX, CO, CA) + **FTC Section 5** deceptive/unfair practices enforcement. | CAC, MIIT, MPS, NRTA: service suspension, rectification orders, administrative penalties. | Follow-up Committee (Art. 23) + domestic remedies (Art. 14). |
