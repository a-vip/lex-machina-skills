# CJEU Teleological Interpretation & Travaux Préparatoires Methodology

## 1. Doctrinal Foundations in European Union Law
Under the jurisprudence of the Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU), statutory interpretation is governed by four interrelated methods:

1. **Literal / Textual Interpretation (Grammatical)**:
   - Analysis of ordinary language, syntax, and punctuation.
   - Multilingual scrutiny: Because EU regulations are authentic in all official languages, discrepancies between language versions must be resolved through contextual and teleological interpretation (*Cilfit*, Case 283/81).

2. **Contextual / Systematic Interpretation**:
   - Examining the position of the article within the chapter, title, and broader legislative architecture.
   - Alignment with horizontal Union standards (e.g., GDPR Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union).
   - Recital alignment: Recitals cast light on the interpretation of legal rules, although they cannot override unambiguous operative provisions (*Nilsson*, Case C-162/97).

3. **Teleological Interpretation (*Effet Utile*)**:
   - Interpreting the provision in light of its overarching objective and regulatory purpose.
   - Provisions creating exceptions or derogations from fundamental protections (e.g., law enforcement exceptions to biometric bans in Article 5(1)(h)) must be **strictly interpreted** (*Kadi*, Joined Cases C-402/05 P and C-415/05 P).

4. **Historical Interpretation (*Travaux Préparatoires*)**:
   - Examining the preparatory acts and negotiating drafts (Commission Proposal, Council amendments, Parliament committee compromises, Trilogue 4-column documents).
   - Establishing why certain phrases were added or excised during trilogue negotiations to clarify legislative intent.

## 2. Standard 4-Column Compromise Framework
In EU legislative trilogues (Commission, Council, Parliament), negotiations center on a 4-Column document:

```text
+-----------------------+-------------------------+------------------------+--------------------------+
| Column 1              | Column 2                | Column 3               | Column 4                 |
| Commission Proposal   | Council General Approach| Parliament Mandate     | Provisional Compromise   |
+-----------------------+-------------------------+------------------------+--------------------------+
| 52021PC0206           | 14954/22                | P9_TA(2023)0236        | ST 5662/24 / 32024R1689  |
+-----------------------+-------------------------+------------------------+--------------------------+
```

When building a legal memorandum:
- **Compare Column 1 vs. Column 3**: Identifies Parliament's policy priorities (e.g., foundation models, environmental reporting, emotion recognition bans).
- **Compare Column 2 vs. Column 3**: Identifies the primary fault lines between Member State security interests and fundamental rights safeguards.
- **Inspect Column 4**: The sealed compromise text containing the precise statutory definitions and derogation conditions.
